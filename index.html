About me

👋Hello, I'm Pingyi.

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at Central South University, advised by Prof. Xianlai Chen. I also work closely with Assoc. Prof. Yunbo Wang at Central South University and Prof. Yuxin Peng at Peking University’s Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology.

My research interests lie in multimodal intelligence, with a focus on vision-language learning, model adaptation, and post-training.

My published and accepted work studies how vision-language models can adapt to biomedical domains with limited supervision and generalize across classes, acquisition conditions, and modalities.

In the longer term, I am interested in unified multimodal models that connect perception, understanding, generation, and action—learning about the world through observation, interaction, and feedback.
